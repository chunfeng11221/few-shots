## Approach

Starting from fancify's merged 103-gate certificate, jointly change the AES input field coordinates, the nine quadratic product planes, and the surrounding linear networks. The resulting circuit has 33 AND, 63 XOR and 6 XNOR gates, for a total of 102 gates. Its maximum output depth is 36. The 18-gate inversion core is retained, including the work credited by the predecessor to HeiGeAi and csbo98.

Let L denote the linear part of the AES affine output map, with its constant 0x63 removed. In the standard AES field modulo 0x11b, use the semilinear input change P(x) = 0x1b * x^16. If the original constant-free S-box output is evaluated at P(x), compensate by the linear output map Q = L composed with Frobenius^4 composed with multiplication by 0x1b composed with L^-1. This follows from inversion of a nonzero field element and also holds at zero. Restore the usual AES affine constant at the output through XNOR gates.

For each of the nine norm products a*b, use orientation 0 = (a,b), 1 = (a,a+b), or 2 = (a+b,b), where addition is XOR. In the order of the predecessor's first nine AND gates, the orientations are [1,2,0,0,2,2,2,1,1]. Over Boolean inputs, a*(a+b) = a*b+a and (a+b)*b = a*b+b; their linear offsets are incorporated in the middle map. Apply the dual column changes to the output map and synthesize the prelinear and transposed output networks together with these choices.

The middle map has four outputs in the predecessor's order [w35,w33,w38,w41]. Its nine norm-product coefficient masks are [4,1,2,14,10,8,3,5,13]. These masks are generated by a five-XOR transposed network. Enumerating invertible four-mask bases reduces the remaining middle-map search to a small linear system with free offset terms. Five already available prelinear functions suffice, in place of the predecessor's six. Their pairs (four-bit coefficient mask, eight-bit input-function mask) are [(4,8),(14,202),(3,34),(5,234),(10,4)]; bit i of an input-function mask denotes x_i.

The gate accounting is:

- 18 prelinear gates;
- 9 norm AND gates;
- 15 middle linear gates (10 from transposing the nine-to-four norm map, plus 5 offset injections);
- 18 inversion-core gates;
- 18 final AND gates;
- 24 output linear gates (the transpose of a 14-gate eight-to-eighteen map).

These sum to 102. Relative to the predecessor, the prelinear layer costs one additional gate, while the middle and output layers each save one gate. The certificate is evaluated on all 256 input bytes by the unchanged official verifier, which reports score "102" and canonical hash b47351b8c602d712efc00ec5cebbd6bdb284e1784ac732f5599bb99f985a0b68. This is an improved upper bound; no optimality claim is made.

## Dead ends

The first search round performed 1,171,086 local root-rewrite attempts on the two published 103-gate circuits, including equal-size substitutions, temporary increases, and up to three-gate replacements. It did not produce a smaller circuit. A generic synthesis attempt for a 17-gate core with exactly six AND gates timed out after 300 seconds, without deciding satisfiability.

Randomized product-plane changes at the fixed starting field representation did not find a qualifying five-offset middle map in the tested candidates. Most arbitrary semilinear input transforms also make the outer linear maps too expensive. Counting required non-input functions in the prelinear and transposed output maps helped focus the search on promising field transforms; joint synthesis then found the certificate above. These failures concern the searched families and heuristics only and are not lower bounds for unrestricted AES circuits.
